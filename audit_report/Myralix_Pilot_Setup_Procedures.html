Key Risks & Mitigation

📌
First Real End-to-End RunAll 7 stages are functionally in place, but this pilot is the first time the full funnel runs against real company data. Treat Day 1's full-funnel walkthrough as validation of the whole chain — stage handoffs are where issues are most likely to surface.
📌
Live Job Board IntegrationsLinkedIn and Jobstreet (Seek) job posting integrations behave differently with live credentials versus test data. Confirm these are tested with realistic job postings on Day 2, not assumed to work because the code path exists.
💡
Team Adoption Across All 7 StagesBecause the full funnel is in scope, the company's team needs to get comfortable with more surface area than a limited pilot would require. Make sure Day 3 training walks the complete Source-to-Onboard journey.
💡
Close Monitoring During Week 1Even fully built features can surface edge cases under real usage patterns. Keep error monitoring active and an engineer reachable through at least the first week of live use, especially around Onboarding checkpoints and Offer approval routing.
